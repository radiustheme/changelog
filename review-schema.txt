* Changed: The FAQ shortcode shows FAQs only from public posts (or posts the visitor can read).

= 3.1.3 (October 17, 2026) =
* Fixed: The [rtrs-review-form] shortcode and the Elementor Review Form widget showed nothing when "Hide Review Form?" was enabled.
* Changed: "Hide Review Summary?", "Hide Review List?" and "Hide Review Form?" now only hide the automatic review section. The [rtrs-review-summary], [rtrs-review-list] and [rtrs-review-form] shortcodes always display, while the existing review rules (one review per user, WooCommerce verified buyers) still apply.

= 3.1.2 (October 13, 2026) =
* Added: Latest AI models — GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and newer GPT-6/5.x models; Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 and Fable 5.1; Gemini 3.8 and 3.6 Flash.
* Changed: AI model lists show the recommended model first and label older models; legacy, preview and deprecated models are removed. Saved selections are kept.
* Changed: New default AI models — Gemini 3.8 Flash and Claude Haiku 5.5. The OpenAI default (GPT-4.1) is now also used when no model is saved.
* Changed: Retiring AI models switch to the provider's replacement on their shutdown date (filter: rtrs_ai_model_shutdowns).
* Changed: On Elementor-built pages, AI tools are used in the Elementor editor; the WordPress editor shows an "Open in Elementor" notice.
* Improved: Gemini 3 models use Google's recommended temperature.
* Fixed: "Failed to fetch schema report" on Elementor pages.
* Fixed: Review notices now show above the review form.
* Fixed: A damaged AI model setting no longer causes an error.

= 3.1.1 (September 24, 2026) =
* Added: Cross-page review display — show an existing post's reviews anywhere with [rtrs-review-list post_id="123"] and [rtrs-review-summary post_id="123"]. The average-rating stars and count shortcodes already accepted post_id.
* Added: "Review Source Post ID" control on the Elementor Review List and Review Summary widgets. Leave it empty to use the current post.
* Improved: Reviews stay owned by their original post — nothing is moved or copied, and the source post's own review settings and layout are used instead of the host page's.
* Improved: Sorting, star filtering and pagination on a cross-page review list act on the selected source post.
* Security: A post_id may only point at a publicly viewable post. Draft, pending, private, trashed and password-protected posts never expose their reviews, and the average-rating shortcodes now enforce the same rule.
* Note: Omitting post_id keeps the existing behaviour exactly — the current post is used. The review form is unchanged and always submits to the page it is placed on.
